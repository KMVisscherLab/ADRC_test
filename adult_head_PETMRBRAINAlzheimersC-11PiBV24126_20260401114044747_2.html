Protocol NotesCube 3D Imaging Considerations: PLEASE DO NOT OBLIQUE. PLEASE DO NOT REDUCE NUMBER OF SLICES. PLEASE DO NOT ADJUST PROTOCOL PARAMETERS. -Use Cube to acquire high resolution imaging in all three dimensions with the goal of acquiring isotropic voxels (all voxel dimensions are equl-height, width, and depth). Consider this information when modifying Cube T2 or Cube T2 FLAIR scan parameters. -Scan selections: 3D Mode, Fast Spine Echo family, Cube or Cube T2 FLAIR pulse, Extended Dynamic Range and IR Prepared Imaging Options. -Typically use Cube to acquire brain, C-spine, pelvis and keee images. -Cube brain scans acquired with User CV Cube Enhance set to 3 (Brain T1) can produce 3D isotropic images with reduced artifacts in comparison to a traditional 2D Spin Echo scan. However, residual flow artifacts and ringing artifact may still be present. -When acquring a Cube pelvis scan, it is recommended you acquire a sagittal or coronal plane. Acquiring an axial plane may result in annefact artifact. -From the scan parameters Details screen, consider selecting Excitation Mode: Non-Selective. -From the scan parameters Details screen, consider selecting Excitation Mode: Focus to redue phase alias artifact when a small FOV is used.
