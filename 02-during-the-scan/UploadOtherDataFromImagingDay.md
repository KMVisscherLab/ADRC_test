This is a quick description of how to upload data collected on the imaging day that isn't imaging data.  https://uab.box.com/s/nrjcc9dq0m5gv6kqtesdld0ch17cj9za <- the box folder with all our data in it

* Data includes Participant Information sheets (PI sheets), which are physical paper.  Extensive notes associated with each participant's data acquisition are included here.  No PHI is included.  When the visit with the participant is complete, the following occurs:
  * a team member from Dr. Visscher's lab will take the PI sheet to be filed in Dr. Visscher's lab (2nd floor, Dr. Jin's office).
  * Once per week, a team member from Dr. Visscher's lab will convert these files to pdf, and upload them to this Box [folder](https://uab.box.com/s/6bh28blduf8qrdw93l6yr2wmssmj676v)
    * obviously these are password protected.
  * When these are uploaded, the Visscher lab team will let the Data Core team know they are uploaded by indicating in [this redcap form](LINK TO REDCAP FORM) that they are ready for further processing.
